Why it exists

Memorizing is one challenge. Keeping it is another.

As your hifz grows, revision becomes the hard part: which pages today, how often, and which ones are starting to fade? Notebooks and memory only go so far. Hifz Companion keeps track for you, so each day you simply open the app and recite.

The right page at the right time

Spaced revision brings each page back just before it would start to weaken.

Weak spots don't hide

Pages you hesitate on get extra attention until you recite them strongly again.

A load you can keep up

Daily capacity limits mean a missed day becomes a manageable catch-up, not a wall.

How it works

Three steps, a few minutes a day

Tell it what you know

Select the pages you have memorized by page, Surah, or complete Juz. The app builds a revision rotation across all of them.

Recite, then rate

Open today's session, recite each page from memory with your own mushaf to hand, and rate how it went.

Let the schedule adapt

Each rating decides when that page comes back. Strong pages wait longer; weak ones return sooner.

Your daily dashboard

Each day's work, sorted into queues

You always know what to recite and why it is there.

Today's Review

Your long-term rotation. Everything you have memorized comes round regularly, about every two weeks by default.

New Memorization

Recently memorized pages, reviewed on a short, tightening schedule until they are stable.

Recovery

Pages rated Weak or Failed, brought back quickly until you recite them strongly twice in a row.

Overdue

Pages past their due date. The daily load is capped, so a backlog is worked through realistically.

Ratings

Rate each recitation honestly

Four simple ratings drive the whole schedule. Rate what you actually recited, not what you know you could.

Strong

Fluent and confident

Waits longer before coming back

Good

Correct, with minor hesitation

Advances normally

Weak

Needed prompting or hesitated repeatedly

Moves to Recovery

Failed

Could not complete it reliably

Starts a dedicated recovery schedule

New pages

Reviewed the next day, then with gaps that grow (2, 3, 4… up to 30 days) until the page joins your long-term rotation.

Weak pages

Come back after 1, 3, then 7 days. Two Strong recitations in a row return them to the rotation.

Failed pages

Revisited the same day, then after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days, until they are strong again.

All of these schedules, the rotation length, and your daily capacity can be changed in Settings.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does the app show the Qur'an text?

No. Hifz Companion is a revision planner: it tells you which pages to recite and keeps track of how each one went. Recite from memory and check against your own mushaf.

Which mushaf does it follow?

Pages follow the 15-line, 604-page Madinah mushaf. Where one Surah ends and the next begins on the same page, the smaller part joins its own Surah's neighbouring page, so each Surah can be revised and logged on its own.

I'm partway through memorizing. Can I still use it?

Yes. Select only the pages you have memorized so far. As you memorize more, log each new page and it starts on the New Memorization schedule.

What happens if I miss a few days?

Missed pages become overdue, but the daily load is capped so you are never handed an impossible session. You catch up over the following days.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. Everything works offline and no sign-up is needed. An internet connection is only used if you choose to back up to Google Drive.

How do I move to a new phone?

Back up to Google Drive from Settings on the old phone, then choose Restore from Cloud when setting up the app on the new one with the same Google account.

Is it available for iPhone?

Not at the moment. Hifz Companion is Android-only and needs Android 8.0 or newer.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. It is free, has no ads or in-app purchases, and the source code is open on GitHub.
